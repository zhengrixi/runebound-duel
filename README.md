A card duel across a lamplit tavern table. You and a rival duelist each hold a
hand of rune cards; theirs lies face down across the table, and a banner reads
their **intent** — the blow (or wall, or mending) their hand is planning — so
every one of your turns is an answer to a threat you can see.

- **Four runes, told apart by colour and shape:** Ember (red flame) burns,
  Aegis (gold shield) guards, Tide (blue wave) draws and mends, Hex (violet
  moon) brands with Burn.
- **Real decisions each hand:** 12 cards that combine — Kindled Rage makes
  Twin Fangs hit twice as hard, Mirror Guard blocks and digs, Hex Brand ticks
  through Block while you hide behind a Bulwark.
- **A ladder of rivals:** Sella the Ash Knight, Brother Cinder, Vey the Tide
  Oracle, the Hollow Regent — beat one, keep your scars (+8 HP), face the next.
- Block fades when your next turn begins, Burn bites at the start of a turn,
  Strength stacks forever. Score is damage dealt plus a speed-and-survival bonus.

Controls: click a card (or press 1–5) to play it · Space ends your turn.
Runs entirely offline from static files in `game/` (`game/index.html` plus its
ES modules under `game/src/`); `main.ts` is the make.fun hand-in program.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts   # then open http://localhost:8080
```
